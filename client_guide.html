3.3 Understanding Cost Price vs Retail Price

FieldDescriptionUsed For
Cost PriceWhat YOU pay the supplier per unitProfit reports, stock valuation
Retail PriceWhat CUSTOMER pays (VAT-inclusive)Selling to walk-in customers
Wholesale PriceDiscounted price for bulk buyersWholesale mode sales

Example:

Sugar 1kg — You buy from supplier at KES 100 (cost), sell at KES 160 (retail).
Your profit per unit = 160 - 100 = KES 60.

When to Update Cost Price:

  • Supplier increases/decreases their price
  • You buy from a different supplier at a different rate
  • You want accurate profit reports
Go to Products
Click Edit on the product
Update the Cost Price field
Click Save Product
Note: When receiving stock, the cost you enter is for that batch only (tracked in stock history). To update the product's main cost price, edit it in the Products page.

Stock Valuation

Your total stock value = (Cost Price × Current Stock) for each product. This is shown on the Dashboard.

Profit Reports

The Profit/Loss report uses Cost Price to calculate:

  • Gross Profit = Revenue − (Cost Price × Quantity Sold)
  • Margin % = (Gross Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100

15. eTIMS Compliance

eTIMS is mandatory for all businesses in Kenya. Receipts must have a QR code to be legally valid.

15.1 What is eTIMS?

eTIMS (electronic Tax Invoice Management System) is KRA's system for electronic tax invoicing. All businesses must issue invoices through eTIMS.

15.2 Required Fields

FieldWhere to Set
Business KRA PINSettings → Store Information
eTIMS UsernameSettings → Store Information
eTIMS PasswordSettings → Store Information
CU SerialSettings → Store Information
Product HS CodesProducts → Add/Edit Product
Product Tax TypesProducts → Add/Edit or Bulk Tax Update
Customer KRA PINCustomers → Add/Edit Customer

15.3 Tax Types

TypeVAT RateExamples
VAT16%Sugar, Oil, Electronics
Exempt0%Bread, Milk, Medicine
Zero-rated0%Exports, Agricultural inputs

15.4 How It Works

Sale is made in POS
Order is saved locally
Invoice is sent to eTIMS (when online)
eTIMS returns: CU Invoice Number, CU Serial, QR Code
Receipt prints with QR code
If offline, transaction is queued and synced later

15.5 eTIMS Sync Status

View current sync status in Settings → eTIMS Sync Status:

  • Synced - Successfully sent to KRA
  • Pending - Waiting to sync
  • Failed - Sync failed, will retry
Click Refresh Status to update the list of recent invoices.

15.6 Tax Filing Support

Even without KRA integration, SokoPulse helps with tax filing:

Go to Reports → Tax Filing
Select your filing period
View VAT 3 return figures (taxable, exempt, zero-rated, VAT collected)
View TOT payable (if applicable)
View Income Tax summary (Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit)
Copy figures directly into iTax when filing
This eliminates manual calculations and reduces filing errors.

15.7 Offline Mode

Your POS works offline:

  • Sales continue as normal
  • Transactions are queued for sync
  • When back online, they sync automatically
  • Check sync status in topbar or Settings

15.6 QR Code

Every receipt has a QR code that contains:

  • Invoice number
  • CU serial
  • Date and amount
  • Business KRA PIN

KRA and customers can scan this to verify the invoice.

17. Troubleshooting

Printer not working?

  • Check printer is connected and turned on
  • Try Browser Print mode as fallback
  • Ensure paper is loaded correctly
  • Some connections require HTTPS (not HTTP)
  • Check browser console (F12) for errors

eTIMS not syncing?

  • Check internet connection
  • Verify eTIMS credentials in Settings
  • Check Settings → eTIMS Sync Status for pending items
  • Ensure business KRA PIN is set
  • Contact your administrator if issues persist

License expired?

  • Contact your administrator for a new activation code
  • Go to Settings → License Activation
  • Enter the new code and click Activate

Products not loading?

  • Refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R)
  • Check browser console (F12) for errors
  • Clear browser cache

Receipt not printing QR code?

  • Ensure eTIMS credentials are configured
  • Check if transaction was synced to eTIMS
  • View Settings → eTIMS Sync Status

Tax not calculating correctly?

  • Verify tax rate in Settings (should be 16 for VAT)
  • Check product tax types in Products page
  • Only VAT products should have tax applied
  • Use Bulk Tax Update to fix multiple products

Customer credit not updating?

  • Credit sales require a registered customer
  • Check credit limit is set correctly
  • View customer details to see current credit
